A LITTLE CONTEXT

Simple rules. A fresh chance.

How to play

Choose Heads or Tails, enter a wager from $0.01 to $100.00, then flip. Quick amounts only fill the field. Each flip uses an independent, unbiased bit from your browser’s secure random source.

A winning $10 wager returns $20 including your $10 stake: a $10 net profit. A loss costs the $10 stake. The $100 cap applies to each wager, even when your balance is larger.

Heads, Tails & the odds

Both sides have a 50/50 chance on every flip. Short sequences need not contain equal numbers of each side. A streak does not predict the next result; your balance, wager, and previous outcomes do not influence it.

Your Safe Bank & optional rewards

Safe Bank stores fictional credits only. Deposit and Withdraw move those credits between Available and Safe Bank at no cost; they never move real money or connect to a bank account. Banked credits never automatically fund a wager. Transfers have no $100 cap and do not change your total fictional credits.

This website does not currently serve live advertisements or rewarded ads. Optional advertising may be added later, with updated disclosures, any required consent, and appropriate provider approval. Any future ad reward would be disclosed before opt-in and would be fictional, nonredeemable game credit. Base winnings never depend on ads.
