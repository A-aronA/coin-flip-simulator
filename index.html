A LITTLE CONTEXT

Simple rules. A fresh chance.

How to play

Choose Heads or Tails, enter a wager from $0.01 to $100.00, then flip. Quick amounts only fill the field. Each flip uses an independent, unbiased bit from your browser’s secure random source.

A winning $10 wager returns $20 including your $10 stake: a $10 net profit. A loss costs the $10 stake. The $100 cap applies to each wager, even when your balance is larger.

Heads, Tails & the odds

Both sides have a 50/50 chance on every flip. Short sequences need not contain equal numbers of each side. A streak does not predict the next result; your balance, wager, and previous outcomes do not influence it.

Your Safe Bank & optional rewards

Move credits between Available and Safe Bank at no cost. Banked credits never automatically fund a wager. Transfers have no $100 cap and do not change your total credits.

When configured and available, optional rewarded ads can add $10 of fictional credit or a disclosed bonus of 1% of a winning gross payout, rounded to the nearest cent. Base winnings never depend on ads. Advertising is disabled by default.

Privacy

This app stores balances, statistics, and recent rounds in your browser’s localStorage. It has no accounts, server wallet, default analytics, or default third-party requests. Browser storage can be edited or cleared, belongs to this origin, and does not sync between devices. It is not authentication or a tamper-proof wallet.

Session-only mode keeps progress in memory until the page closes. If advertising is enabled later, providers may process additional data; deployment requires an appropriate provider/CMP integration and updated disclosures.

Terms & fictional credits

For entertainment, intended for adults 18+. Fictional credits only. No purchases, cash value, transfers to other people, prizes, redemption, or real-money gambling. Safe Bank transfers occur only within this local game. Progress may be lost if browser data is cleared. The 18+ notice is not a substitute for any publisher-required age controls.
